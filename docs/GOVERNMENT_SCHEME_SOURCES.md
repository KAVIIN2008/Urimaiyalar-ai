## 2. Scheme-by-Scheme Grounded Parameters

### A. NEEDS (New Entrepreneur-cum-Enterprise Development Scheme)
- **Authority:** Department of MSME, Government of Tamil Nadu
- **Subsidy:** 25% of the project cost.
- **Ceiling:** Maximum ₹75,00,000 (₹75 Lakhs).
- **Interest Subvention:** 3% back-ended interest subvention across loan tenure.
- **Eligible Cost Range:** ₹10,00,000 to ₹5,00,00,000 (₹5 Crores).
- **Education:** Degree, Diploma, ITI, or accredited Vocational Training.
- **Age Window:** 21 to 35 years (relaxed to 45 for Women, SC/ST, BC, MBC, Minorities, Differently-Abled, Ex-Servicemen).
- **First-Gen Requirement:** Applicant must be a First-Generation Entrepreneur.
- **Sectors:** Manufacturing and Service (Trading excluded).

### B. UYEGP (Unemployed Youth Employment Generation Programme)
- **Authority:** District Industries Centres (DIC), Government of Tamil Nadu
- **Subsidy:** 25% of the project cost.
- **Ceiling:** Maximum ₹3,75,000 for Manufacturing (25% of ₹15L); ₹1,25,000 for Service/Business.
- **Eligible Cost Range:** ₹1,00,000 to ₹15,00,000 for Manufacturing; Up to ₹5,00,000 for Service/Trading.
- **Education:** Minimum 8th Standard Pass.
- **Income Ceiling:** Family annual income must not exceed ₹5,00,000.
- **Age Window:** 18 to 35 years (General), up to 45 years (Special Categories).
- **Sectors:** Manufacturing, Service, and Trading (Retail Business eligible).

### C. PMEGP (Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme)
- **Authority:** Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC), Ministry of MSME, Govt of India
- **Subsidy (Margin Money):**
  - Urban General: 15% (Own contribution: 10%)
  - Urban Special: 25% (Own contribution: 5%)
  - Rural General: 25% (Own contribution: 10%)
  - Rural Special: 35% (Own contribution: 5%)
- **Ceiling:** Up to ₹17,50,000 (35% of ₹50 Lakhs).
- **Eligible Cost Range:** Up to ₹50,00,000 for Manufacturing; Up to ₹20,00,000 for Service.
- **Age Window:** Minimum 18 years. No upper age ceiling.
- **Education:** 8th pass required for projects > ₹10L (Mfg) and > ₹5L (Service).
